THE STUDIO

Hi, I'm Mara — I shoot Wren & Field.

I started Wren & Field after seven years shooting editorial work for regional magazines. I bring the same instinct here: quiet observation over posed perfection, natural light over flash, real moments over a shot list.

I shoot around 18 weddings a year so that every one gets full attention — from the first call through the delivered gallery, it's just me, sometimes with a second shooter for larger days.

— Mara Ellison
