QUESTIONS

A few things people ask.

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9–14 months out, especially for Saturdays in peak season (May–October). I only take 18 weddings a year, so popular dates go quickly.

Do you travel outside the Hudson Valley?

Yes — I regularly shoot in the greater Northeast and am open to destination weddings. Travel outside a 50-mile radius includes a travel fee, quoted case by case.

How long until we get our photos?

A full gallery is delivered within 6–8 weeks, with a set of 20–30 sneak peeks shared within the first week.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. A 30% retainer secures your date, with the balance split into two or three payments leading up to the wedding.
