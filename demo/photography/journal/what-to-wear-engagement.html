What to Wear to Your Engagement Session

September 2, 2025 · Planning Guides

The single most common question I get before an engagement session isn't about location or timing — it's "what do we wear?" Here's the short version: dress like slightly nicer versions of yourselves, and coordinate rather than match.

Coordinate, Don't Match

Matching outfits (same color, same pattern) tend to flatten a photo. Coordinating — sticking to a shared palette of two or three complementary tones — gives the eye something to move between without feeling staged.

Texture Over Logos

Linen, knits, and soft layers photograph beautifully, especially outdoors. Avoid large logos, busy patterns, or anything you'll be tugging at all session — if it's not comfortable in the mirror, it won't be comfortable two hours in.

Bring a Backup

A second outfit gives us a natural way to shift the mood of the session — dressier for a golden-hour close, more relaxed for the walking portion. It's not required, but it's an easy way to get two distinct looks out of one session.

Trust the Location

If we're shooting somewhere with a strong color palette — a red barn, a green orchard — keep your outfits a little more neutral so the location gets to do some of the work.
