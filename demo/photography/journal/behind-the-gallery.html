BEHIND THE SCENES

From Shutter to Gallery: My Editing Process

July 14, 2025 · Behind the Scenes

A wedding day produces somewhere between 2,500 and 4,000 frames. Here's what happens to them between the moment the shutter clicks and the moment your gallery link lands in your inbox.

The First Pass: Culling

Within a day or two of the wedding, I go through everything once to remove blinks, duplicates, and test frames — usually cutting the set down by more than half. This is the least glamorous part of the process and also the most important; a tight, well-edited gallery tells your story better than an exhaustive one.

Color & Light

Next comes a full edit pass on every remaining image — exposure, white balance, and the color grading that gives a gallery its consistent look from ceremony through reception, regardless of how the light changed across the day.

The Sneak Peek

Around 20–30 favorites get a priority edit and go out within the first week — something to hold you over and share with family while the full gallery is still in progress.

Delivery

The complete gallery, typically 500–700 images depending on coverage length, is delivered within 6–8 weeks through a private online gallery with full-resolution downloads and a print release.
