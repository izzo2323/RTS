REAL WEDDING

An October Wedding at Rhinecliff

October 18, 2025 · Real Weddings

Elena and Sam found their venue by accident — a wrong turn on a drive upstate led them past the old rail depot in Rhinecliff, and eight months later they were married on its lawn overlooking the Hudson.

What I loved most about this day was how little of it was performed. There was no grand entrance, no choreographed first look for an audience — just Sam turning around at the sound of Elena's footsteps on the gravel path, and both of them laughing before either said a word.

The Ceremony

Golden hour landed almost exactly on the vows, which is the kind of luck you can't plan for no matter how carefully you build a timeline. The officiant, a family friend, kept things short — twelve minutes, by my count — which left more daylight for portraits along the river.

The Reception

Inside the depot's old freight room, string lights and a single long table replaced the usual round-table reception layout. Toasts ran long, in the best way. By the time the band played its last song, half the guest list was still on the dance floor in coats, having spilled out onto the platform to cool off between sets.

Congratulations again to Elena and Sam — thank you for trusting me with the day.
