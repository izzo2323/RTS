HOW I SHOOT

Quiet observation over posed perfection.

I started Wren & Field after seven years shooting editorial work for regional magazines. I bring the same instinct here: natural light over flash, real moments over a shot list, and a documentary eye that trusts the day to give me what I need.

I shoot around 18 weddings a year so that every one gets full attention — from the first call through the delivered gallery, it's just me, sometimes with a second shooter for larger days. Between weddings, I shoot engagement sessions, family portraits, and the occasional editorial project.

I'm based in Rhinebeck but travel throughout the Hudson Valley and beyond — ask if your venue is outside the area, travel is almost always workable.

— Mara Ellison
