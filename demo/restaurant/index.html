OUR HEARTH

One fire, one menu, no shortcuts.

Ember & Oak has no gas line to the kitchen. Every dish that leaves the pass has touched the hearth — a single, custom-built oak and hickory fire that our cooks tend from open to close.

Chef Dana Whitfield spent six years cooking over open flame in Northern California before bringing the format to Millbrook in 2021. The restaurant seats fifty, sources from four regional farms, and closes the kitchen the moment the wood runs low — not a minute after.

We don't do tasting menus or wine pairings with a script. We do a short list of things cooked well, and a room that feels like someone's kitchen table.
