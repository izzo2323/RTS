ABOUT

Hi, I'm Nick Rizzo.

I started Rizzo Technology Solutions because most small businesses end up choosing between two bad options: an overpriced agency that treats them like a line item, or a freelancer who disappears once the invoice clears.

I wanted to build something else — a single person you can actually call, who understands both the code behind your website and the network humming in your back office. Most businesses end up with a web developer who doesn't touch hardware and an IT contractor who won't touch code. I do both, so nothing falls into the gap between two vendors pointing fingers at each other.

Every engagement starts the same way: I ask what's actually slowing you down, not what package I'd like to sell you. Sometimes that's a five-page website. Sometimes it's a two-hour phone call that saves you from a $10,000 mistake.

If it plugs in, connects to the internet, or has a URL, I can probably help.
