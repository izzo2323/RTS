WHAT I DO

Two disciplines, one point of contact.

Most businesses end up with a web developer who doesn't touch hardware and an IT contractor who won't touch code. I do both, so you call one number instead of two — and nothing falls into the gap between two vendors pointing fingers at each other.

SYSTEMS

IT Consulting & Support

Practical help with the technology running your business day to day — sized to a small business, not sold like an enterprise contract.

  • Network setup & troubleshooting — wired and wireless networks that work reliably, diagnosed and fixed when they don't.
  • Cybersecurity checkups — a plain-language audit of where you're exposed, and what to fix first.
  • Cloud migration guidance — moving files, email, or backups off aging local hardware without losing anything.
  • Hardware & software procurement advice — what to actually buy, before you spend money on the wrong thing.
  • Technology roadmapping — a simple plan for what to upgrade or replace over the next 12 months.
WEB

Web Development

Custom-built sites and web apps — clean code, fast load times, and a site you can actually log in and update yourself afterward.

  • Custom business websites — built for your business, not assembled from a generic template.
  • Redesigns & modernization — bringing an aging or slow site up to current standards without starting from zero.
  • Web applications — booking systems, payment integrations, or custom internal tools.
  • Self-editable builds — a CMS you can update without calling me every time the phone number changes.
  • Performance & SEO fundamentals — fast pages, clean markup, and the basics search engines actually reward.
SUPPORT
