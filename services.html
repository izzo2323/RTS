WHAT I DO

Four ways to work together, one point of contact.

Most businesses end up with a web developer who doesn't touch hardware and an IT contractor who won't touch code. I do both — one-off or ongoing, remote or on-site — so nothing falls into the gap between two vendors pointing fingers at each other.

SYSTEMS

IT Consulting & Support

Hands-on help with the technology running your business day to day — remote, on-site, or on-call when something can't wait.

  • Remote IT Support — troubleshooting, Microsoft 365, software, and network issues handled without a truck roll.
  • On-Site IT Support — hardware installs, network setup, new computer deployment, and anything that needs hands on equipment.
  • Advanced IT Consulting — servers, cloud migrations, networking, and security projects that need deeper infrastructure expertise.
  • Emergency & After-Hours Support — evenings, weekends, and business-critical outages that need an immediate response.
  • IT Infrastructure Assessments — a full review of your systems with a written list of findings and recommendations.
MANAGED IT

Managed IT Services

Recurring, proactive support instead of paying for every request one at a time — plans that scale from basic monitoring to a fully outsourced IT department.

  • Remote monitoring & patching — endpoints kept current and watched, not discovered broken.
  • Unlimited standard remote support — included on Business and Complete plans.
  • Microsoft 365 & security administration — user accounts, email, and endpoint security managed for you.
  • Network & backup monitoring — so a failure gets caught before it becomes downtime.
  • Quarterly technology reviews — a regular check-in on what's working and what's next.
  • Priority response & vendor management — on the Complete plan, for businesses that want RTS to function as their IT department.
WEB

Web Development

Custom-built sites and web apps — clean code, fast load times, and a site you can actually log in and update yourself afterward.

  • Starter, Business & Professional site tiers — from a clean 5-page presence to a fully custom, 20-page build with advanced integrations.
  • Custom web applications — customer portals, booking systems, CRM integrations, and other custom functionality.
  • E-commerce — online stores, from a small product catalog to advanced multi-integration builds.
  • SEO & performance fundamentals — clean markup, fast pages, and the basics search engines reward, built in from the start.
  • Content migration & revisions — moving an existing site over, with multiple rounds of revisions built into every project.
CARE

Website Care & Hosting

So the site doesn't quietly go stale after launch — hosting, updates, and a standing block of time for small changes.

  • Daily backups & uptime monitoring — tested backups and a watchful eye on whether the site is actually up.
