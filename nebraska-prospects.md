# Nebraska Prospect Research — Statewide, No-Website Businesses

Compiled Aug 2026. 38 independently-owned small/medium businesses across Nebraska, originally verified to have **no owned website** — their only online presence is Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, a booking-platform page (Booksy/Fresha/GlossGenius), or a free auto-generated Google Business Profile page. Email addresses added in a second pass, sourced from Facebook "Contact info" sections, BBB profiles, and directory listings — **18 of 38 have a verified email; the rest are marked "not found" rather than guessed.** Never invent an `info@business.com`-style address for the "not found" ones.

Categories match what's worked in the Omaha batch: barbershops/salons, tattoo shops, auto detailing/repair, food trucks/restaurants, landscaping, and pet grooming.

**Three corrections surfaced during email research — read before sending anything:**
- **Little Bear Outdoor Services (Lincoln)** and **McCarts Lawn N More (Lincoln)** actually do have owned websites (littlebearoslincoln.com and a Wix site respectively) — the original no-website screen missed these. Left in the list with emails since they're still real businesses, but the "no website" pitch angle doesn't apply — reposition as an upgrade pitch or drop them.
- **Showroom Auto Detailing (Fremont)** — their own Facebook post (Apr 2025) says the owner is "officially closing Showroom Auto Detailing for good." May no longer be operating — confirm before any outreach.
- **Twizted Needle Tattoo (North Platte)** — the address/phone on file now shows up under a different name, "The Parlor Tattoo Studio," on Google's current listing. Possible rebrand — confirm before outreach.

Where a rating, phone, address, or email wasn't confirmable, it's marked **not found**. A few entries are flagged **lower confidence** where verification was incomplete — do a quick manual check before drafting outreach to those.

Businesses that turned out to already have an owned website were excluded during research and aren't listed here (noted briefly per region for context).
