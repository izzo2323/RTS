# Nebraska Outreach Emails — Statewide Batch

Drafted Aug 2026, companion to `nebraska-prospects.md`. Unlike the original Omaha batch, **no demo sites exist yet for any of these 38 businesses** — these are first-touch cold emails asking whether they'd be interested in a website, not links to something already built. No pricing is mentioned anywhere on purpose — that's held back for when they reach out. Openers lead with something specific about the business (a story, a detail, an observation), not a review count or star rating.

Every email now includes a short personal-intro line — who Nick is, that Rizzo Technology Solutions is a small, newly-started web design business in Omaha, and that he's reaching out directly rather than running ads. Four openers were also fixed for a logical contradiction: they'd said "I was looking around and found you easily," then immediately claimed "new customers can't find you," which reads as self-defeating. Fixed by attributing Nick's discovery specifically to Facebook (Seven Seas, Felix Barbershop, Patino's Barber Shop, The Fade Away Barbershop) — consistent with "Facebook is the only place to find you," rather than implying a general web search worked fine.

**Not drafted / need action first:**
- **Little Bear Outdoor Services** and **McCarts Lawn N More** (Lincoln) — both already have websites. Wrong angle for this batch; reposition as an upgrade pitch or drop.
- **Showroom Auto Detailing** (Fremont) — their own Facebook post says they closed for good. Skipped.
- **Twizted Needle Tattoo** (North Platte) — drafted below, but **verify first**: the address/phone now shows up under a different name, "The Parlor Tattoo Studio," on Google's current listing. Possible rebrand — confirm before sending.

**Delivery note:** 15 of these 35 have a verified email address (send normally). The other 20 are marked "No email found" — adapt the copy for a Facebook Message or use it as a phone-call talking-point instead.
