COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Do I need to sign a contract?+

Yes, but it's a short one-page agreement covering scope, timeline, and payment terms — not a 20-page legal document. The standard warranty, confidentiality, and legal terms live on our Standard Terms of Service page, which you don't sign separately. You'll see everything before any work starts, and nothing locks you into future work.

Who owns the site once it's finished?+

You do. Once the final invoice is paid, the code, content, and any accounts created for the project are yours — no ongoing licensing fee to keep your own site running.

What if my project doesn't fit neatly into a tier?+

Most don't fit perfectly, which is normal. The tiers above are starting points for the consult, not a menu you have to pick from exactly — the free 20-minute call is where we figure out the real scope and a real number.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes. Project-based work generally requires a deposit before starting, with the remaining balance due as the project progresses — exact terms are laid out in your proposal. Managed IT and website care plans are billed monthly.

What's not included in the listed price?+

Third-party costs like domain registration, software licenses, or hardware are billed separately at cost, apart from the base price. You'll know about any of these before they're purchased — nothing gets added to your bill as a surprise.
