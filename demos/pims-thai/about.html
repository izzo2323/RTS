OUR STORY

Five years in, and just getting started.

Pim's Thai spent five years serving some of the best Thai food in Omaha from its takeout counter, built by owner Waraporn around her own family recipes.

Today, Pim's Thai has taken those recipes on the road — operating as a dedicated catering company and a mobile food truck, making it Omaha's only Thai catering business. You'll find the truck at community events and campuses around the metro, and the catering side is booking private and corporate events.

Vegetarian substitutions are available on most dishes — just ask when you book.
