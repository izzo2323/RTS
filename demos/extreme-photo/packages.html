QUESTIONS

A few things people ask.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, 6–12 months out is typical, especially for weekend dates in peak season (May–October). Senior and family sessions can often be booked with just a few weeks' notice.

Do you travel outside West Des Moines?

Yes — sessions across the greater Des Moines and Central Iowa area are standard. Travel further out is available, quoted case by case.

How long until we get our photos?

Galleries are typically delivered within 2–4 weeks, with retouching included at no extra charge.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. A deposit secures your date, with the remaining balance due before the session or split into installments — ask for details.
