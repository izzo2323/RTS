HOW I SHOOT

Contemporary, natural, photojournalistic — whatever the day calls for.

I've been in the photography business for more than 35 years, starting with a degree from the University of Northern Iowa. In that time I've shot weddings, senior portraits, family sessions, engagements, and everything in between across Central Iowa.

My approach blends contemporary, natural, and traditional photojournalistic styles rather than locking every client into one look. Every session includes complimentary retouching — it's built into the price, not an add-on.

I believe you shouldn't have to choose between quality and an honest price. That's the whole idea behind Extreme Photo: guaranteed top-quality images you'll want to keep for a lifetime, without big-studio markup.

— William McVicker
