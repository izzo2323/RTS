This demo uses CSS-gradient placeholder blocks instead of real photos (see .hero-art
and .about-art in style.css), since no rights-cleared photos of the actual cafe were
used for this mockup.

To finish the site for real use, add:
- hero.jpg — a wide shot of the dining room or a signature dish (used in place of .hero-art)
- about.jpg — an interior or team photo (used in place of .about-art)

Free stock options if real photos aren't available yet: Unsplash, Pexels, or Pixabay
(search "diner breakfast" / "cozy cafe interior").
