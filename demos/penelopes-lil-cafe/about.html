OUR STORY

A little cafe, run like family.

Penelope's Lil' Cafe opened its doors in 2014 as a small, family-owned breakfast spot in a Lincoln neighborhood — and it's stayed that way ever since. No corporate menu, no drive-thru, just a handful of tables and a kitchen that treats every plate like it's for a guest at home.

Regulars know the cafe for its all-day breakfast, hearty portions, and a staff that remembers your order before you've finished sitting down. It's the kind of place neighbors describe as "the cutest little cafe around."

Over a decade later, the cafe is still run day-to-day by the family that started it — open Monday through Friday, mornings through early afternoon.
