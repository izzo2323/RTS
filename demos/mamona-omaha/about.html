OUR STORY

A truck named after my mother.

Ma' Mona — “my mother” in Spanish — opened in 2023, built around the recipes of Margarita Gonzalez, who brought her family's Jalisco cooking straight from the kitchen to the parking lot at 3536 Q Street.

Nothing here is pre-made. Tortillas are pressed by hand, salsas are built fresh each morning, and the Vampiro taco — griddled cheese, your choice of meat, house salsa — has become the dish people drive across Omaha for.

We're open seven days a week, right in the NP Mart lot, with weekend specials like handmade menudo and tamales that sell out fast.
